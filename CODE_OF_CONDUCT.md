# Code of Conduct

LlamaPReview welcomes contributors and users with different backgrounds,
experience levels, identities, and viewpoints. Project spaces should remain
useful places to improve code review through evidence and respectful technical
discussion.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas, code, and evidence rather than people.
- Make room for questions and good-faith disagreement.
- Acknowledge mistakes and help repair their impact.
- Protect other people's privacy and confidential information.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized attention or imagery.
- Doxxing or publishing private source, credentials, logs, identities, or
  communications without permission.
- Sustained disruption, spam, impersonation, or knowingly misleading reports.
- Retaliation against someone who raises a good-faith concern.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, Discussions, and other
project-controlled spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close or lock
threads, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when necessary to protect the community.

For a sensitive conduct report, contact the maintainer privately at
Jiantong.xu@foxmail.com. Include only the information needed to understand the
incident. Reports will be reviewed as privately as practical, but the project
does not promise a response deadline or absolute confidentiality.
